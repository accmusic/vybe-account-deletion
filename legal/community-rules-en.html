Lel Community Rules

Lel is a place to share music. You must be 18 or older to use it.

Not allowed

  1. Harassment and bullying: insults, threats, humiliating or stalking someone, posting other people’s personal data.
  2. Hate: attacking people for their origin, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or other characteristics.
  3. Sexual content: nudity and explicit sexual content. Any sexual content involving minors is prohibited without exception and is reported to the authorities (see “Child Safety Standards”).
  4. Violence: threats, incitement to violence or self-harm, glorifying crimes.
  5. Impersonation: pretending to be another person or an artist. Fan pages are run by fans and must not present themselves as official artist pages.
  6. Copyright: Lel hosts no audio; don’t share links to illegal copies.
  7. Spam and scams: repeated messages, unwanted advertising, misleading links or fraud.
  8. Accounts of people under 18 and accounts created to get around a ban.
  9. Private messages — the same rules apply: no harassment, spam or unsolicited sexual content.

How to report

Every post, comment, playlist, profile, discussion topic and reply, private message and conversation has “Report”. Choose a reason; for risks to children use “Child safety”. If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services. You can also block anyone.

What we do

We review urgent child safety reports within 24 hours and all other reports within 7 days. Depending on severity, we remove content, limit the account or ban it. A banned account can’t post or comment and no longer appears in lists and search. To appeal, write to accmusic.support@gmail.com.

Fan page owners and moderators can remove posts, comments, topics and replies on their page, close and pin topics, and turn off discussions.
