Lel Child Safety Standards

Version date: November 1, 2026.

Lel has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Prohibited

  • Any image, text or link that sexualizes minors.
  • Contacting a minor for sexual purposes (grooming), asking them for images or meetings.
  • Sexual blackmail (sextortion) and threats to share intimate images.
  • Sharing, seeking or promoting child sexual abuse material.

How we protect minors

  • Minimum age: 18. When signing up, people confirm they are 18 or older; accounts of people under 18 are blocked as soon as we learn about it and deleted after 7 days.
  • Anyone can report a profile that looks like it belongs to someone under 18.
  • Private messages exist. By default, only people who follow each other can write to each other; a person can let followers send a message request or block new conversations. Conversations include report and block options. If a message is reported, moderators see only that message; if a conversation is reported, they see the last 10 messages from the reported person. They do not see the rest of the conversation.

How to report

In the app: “Report” → “Child safety” on any post, comment, playlist, profile, discussion topic or reply, private message or conversation. By email: accmusic.support@gmail.com. If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.

What we do with a report

  • Review: urgent child safety reports within 24 hours, all others within 7 days.
  • Removal of the content and ban of the account.
  • Preservation of evidence for 1 year, or longer at the request of law enforcement.
  • We report child sexual abuse material to the Cyberpolice of Ukraine and to NCMEC (CyberTipline), and to law enforcement in the country of the user at their request.

Child safety contact

Child safety contact: accmusic.support@gmail.com. This contact answers authorities and Google Play about child safety.
