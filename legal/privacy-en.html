Lel Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026.

Who is responsible

Data controller: Andrii Horiachkin, Kyiv, Ukraine. Contact: accmusic.support@gmail.com.

What data we process and why

You can turn push off in Settings → Notifications or in Android settings.

On your device

The app stores on the phone, encrypted and excluded from backups, the sessions and basic data (email, username, name) of up to 5 added accounts. They are removed when you sign out of that account or delete it. The last 8 search queries of each account are also kept only on this phone; you can delete them in Search (“Clear”), and they are removed together with the account on this device.

Who we share data with

  • Database, authentication and storage provider: Supabase (servers in the United States, East US region, North Virginia).
  • Email delivery (confirmation codes): Brevo.
  • Music catalogs: Apple (iTunes Search API) and MetaBrainz (MusicBrainz) receive only the search text or the song identifier.
  • Google Play: app distribution.
  • Push notifications: Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) receives the push token of the phone and the content of each push (type of event and reaction, username and, only if you enable it, a short text and the discussion topic title). We use only Firebase Cloud Messaging, without Firebase analytics or advertising. For direct messages, the push includes the sender’s username and, only if you turn it on, up to 100 characters of the text.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who sees your content

Your profile and posts are visible according to your settings (public or private profile).

Your reactions to posts are seen by everyone who can see the post: they see the number of reactions of each kind and, in the “Reactions” list, who reacted and with which reaction (people you blocked or who blocked you are not shown).

“Favorites” on your profile: up to three fan pages, playlists and songs per section — the ones you chose or, until you choose, your fan pages, your latest public playlists and your latest favorite songs. It is seen by everyone who can see your profile (except people you blocked or who blocked you); nobody receives anything beyond what is shown. Your favorite songs (the ones you hearted) are seen by the people you pick in “Who sees your favorite tracks”: mutual followers, followers (default), everyone or nobody. You can change this on your profile: the pencil next to “Favorites”. The total number of likes of a song is visible to everyone, without names.

“Now playing” is seen by the audience you choose: mutual followers (default), followers, everyone or nobody. You choose it on the screen where you turn the feature on and can change it later in Settings → Music. People you blocked or who blocked you never see it. We keep only your current status, with no listening history. To turn it off: Settings → Music → “Show what I’m listening to”, or remove Lel’s notification access in Android settings. Turning it off, signing out or switching accounts deletes your status from our server right away.

Direct messages

  • Who sees them. Only you and the person you’re talking to.
  • “Read” mark. While a conversation is open on your screen, we save up to which message you have seen it. The other person sees “Read” under their messages only if both of you have “Read receipts” on (on by default); if either of you turns it off, neither of you sees the other’s “Read”. There is no mark in a message request that hasn’t been accepted or after a block. In a message request there is no mark until it’s accepted, and messages read before that aren’t marked afterwards. To turn it off: Settings → Privacy and security → Messages → “Read receipts”.
  • “Typing…”. While a conversation is open on your screen, the app lets the other person know that you are typing or choosing a sticker or an emoji (they also see it in their list of conversations). This signal goes through our real-time service (Supabase Realtime) only to the other participant of an accepted conversation, disappears from the screen within a few seconds and isn’t kept with your messages: only the conversation and the type of signal pass through the service’s internal queue, which Supabase deletes automatically within a few days; it isn’t sent after a block. The indicator also works both ways: the other person sees it only if both of you have “Typing indicator” on (on by default); turn it off and you won’t see theirs either. Not shown in a message request until it’s accepted. To turn it off: Settings → Privacy and security → Messages → “Typing indicator”.
  • Encryption. Messages are stored on our servers (Supabase) and travel over a secure connection (HTTPS), but without end-to-end encryption: technically, Lel as the service operator can access them. We don’t read your conversations or use them for ads or recommendations.
  • Moderation. Moderators see messages only after a report: a report on a message includes a copy of that message; a report on a conversation or message request includes a copy of the reported person’s last 10 messages (text and markers for forwarded posts and stickers). Moderators don’t see any other messages.
  • Who can message you. By default, only mutual follows. In Settings → Privacy and security → “Who can message me” you can let your followers send a message request or block new conversations (“No one”; accepted conversations stay). Blocked people can’t message you.
  • Deleting. “Delete for everyone” immediately removes the message text, post and sticker for both of you (a “Message deleted” marker stays). “Delete conversation” only hides it for you. When you or the other person deletes their account, the conversation and all its messages are deleted for both.
  • Push. A message push includes the sender’s username and, only if you turn on “Text on the lock screen”, up to 100 characters of the text; a message request push has no text. To turn it off: Settings → Notifications → “Messages (push)”.

How long we keep data

  • Account and content: until you delete your account.
  • Notifications: 90 days.
  • Push token: while push is on for that account on this phone; deleted when you turn push off, sign out, switch to another account (the token moves to it), delete the account, or when Firebase reports it is no longer valid (for example, after the app is removed). One account keeps at most 10 tokens; older ones are deleted.
  • Posts you have seen in the feed: 30 days.
  • “Now playing”: only the latest status, up to 6 hours; deleted sooner when you turn the feature off, sign out, switch accounts or delete your account.
  • Reports with a copy of the content: up to 90 days after the reported account is deleted; child safety reports: 1 year from the decision, longer if required by law or a law enforcement request.
  • Direct messages: until you or the other person deletes their account (the conversation is then deleted for both) or until the sender deletes the message “for everyone”. Message copies in reports follow the reports item.
  • Username of a deleted account: reserved for 30 days to prevent impersonation (no other data).
  • Provider backups: our current plan does not include scheduled backups.
  • Replaced profile photos: the app deletes the previous photo right after it is replaced. If that deletion fails, the file may stay on the server until you delete your account.

Deleted topics and replies in fan page discussions disappear for everyone, but we keep them hidden for 1 year to review reports and then delete them. If you delete your account, the text of your topics and replies is erased immediately; only copies included in reports, if any, remain for up to 1 year.

Deleting your account

Immediate and permanent: in the app (Profile → ☰ → Settings → “Delete account”), on the web at https://accmusic.github.io/vybe-account-deletion/ or by email to accmusic.support@gmail.com. Other people’s replies to your comments are kept and your comment shows as deleted. Your discussion replies show as “Reply deleted” and their text is erased. Your topics without replies from others are deleted; in topics with replies from others, the title, text and attachments are erased and those replies are kept. Your direct conversations are deleted for both people.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict its processing, data portability, object to processing and withdraw your consent. To use these rights, write to accmusic.support@gmail.com. You can also complain to the data protection authority of your country: in Ukraine, the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights; in Spain, the AEPD.

Minors

Lel is only for people aged 18 and over. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we block it right away and delete it after 7 days; during that time the person can write to us if it’s a mistake. A blocked account can’t confirm its age again. Anyone can report a profile that looks like it belongs to someone under 18.

Changes

We will announce important changes to this policy in the app.
